spilr.

A private journaling app for iPhone.

Spilr is a place to get what is on your mind out of your head. Write a few lines, or talk through your day in a short chat, and Spilr turns it into a journal entry that stays in your account.

What you can do with Spilr

  • Journal your way — a blank page, guided templates, or a timed writing session.
  • Daily Chat — answer a few questions about your day and Spilr weaves your answers into an entry.
  • Reflections on your own words — Today's Read, Echoes, Mirror and Patterns point out themes that keep coming up in what you write. AI reflections are optional and can be switched off in Settings.
  • Your calendar alongside your journal — optionally see your Google Calendar events in Spilr's Calendar view (details below).

Private by design

Your entries are yours. Nobody else can see them, there is no social feed, and we do not sell your data, show ads, or use your writing to train AI models. You can delete everything, permanently, from Settings.

How Spilr uses Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and off by default. If you connect it, Spilr asks for read-only access to your calendar events (calendar.events.readonly) for one purpose: to show your events inside Spilr's Calendar view, so you can see your day next to your journal.

  • Spilr never creates, edits or deletes anything on your calendar.
  • Events are fetched live from Google when you open the Calendar view and are kept only in memory on your device. They are not stored on our servers, not shared with anyone, and not sent to any AI model.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Spilr, or remove access from your Google Account permissions page.

Spilr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
