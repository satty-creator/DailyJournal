❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: Will this slow down my app?
A: No. <10ms per session, unnoticeable. Firebase SDK is optimized.

Q: Will this break existing features?
A: No. Completely non-intrusive, fire-and-forget, backwards compatible.
   See ANALYTICS_SAFETY.md for proof.

Q: Do I need new third-party tools?
A: No. Uses Firebase Analytics (free, already in your project).

Q: Can I remove it later?
A: Yes. Delete tracking calls, app works identically.

Q: Does this log personal data?
A: No. Only aggregated metrics (screen names, events, durations).
   Entry content, email, etc. are never logged.

Q: What if Firebase is down?
A: Analytics silently fails. App works normally. No user impact.

🔑 KEY METRICS TO WATCH

Weekly:
  • DAU (daily active users) - growing?
  • D7 Retention - >25%?
  • Entries per day - stable?

Monthly:
  • D1/D7/D30 Retention curves
  • Funnel conversions (signup → entry)
  • Feature adoption (% using each feature)

Red flags:
  • DAU drops >50% in 1 day → investigate
  • Crash rate >1% → rollback
  • Feature adoption <20% → redesign or cut
