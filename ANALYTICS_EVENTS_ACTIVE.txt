🎯 EXAMPLE QUERIES YOU CAN RUN IN FIREBASE

Question: "What % of signups complete at least one entry?"
Query: Create Funnel
  Step 1: signup_completed
  Step 2: entry_created
  Result: Completion rate %

Question: "Do AI users have higher retention?"
Query: Create Cohort
  Cohort A: event ai_insights_generated
  Cohort B: no ai_insights_generated
  Compare: D1/D7/D30 retention

Question: "What's the average entry composition time?"
Query: Events
  Filter: entry_created
  Parameter: composition_time_seconds
  Stat: average, p50, p95

Question: "Which sessions result in entry creation?"
Query: Events
  Filter: session_started → entry_created sequence
  Stat: Conversion rate

Question: "How many entries include mood?"
Query: Events
  Filter: entry_created with has_mood = true
  Stat: % of total entries

✨ WHAT'S TRACKED PER EVENT

entry_created:
  • session_type: "freeWrite" | "timed" | "dailyChat" | "cbtReframe"
  • word_count: integer (0-5000+)
  • has_mood: boolean
  • has_photo: boolean
  • has_ai_insights: boolean
  • composition_time_seconds: integer
  • timestamp: ISO8601

ai_insights_generated:
  • entry_type: "freeWrite" | etc
  • bullet_count: 0-5+
  • has_question: boolean
  • latency_ms: 0-5000+
  • timestamp: ISO8601

mood_logged:
  • mood: "amazing" | "good" | "neutral" | "bad" | "terrible"
  • timestamp: ISO8601

session_started:
  • days_after_signup: 0-365+
  • timestamp: ISO8601

session_ended:
  • session_duration_seconds: 0-3600+
  • entries_written: 0-100+
  • timestamp: ISO8601

login_completed / signup_completed:
  • provider: "email" | "google" | "apple"
  • timestamp: ISO8601
